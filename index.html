About

Where health
meets technology

A multi-disciplinary leader whose work spans global health strategy, pharmacology, health systems strengthening, software development, and education.

Eloho bridges the gap between clinical science and digital tools that can transform healthcare delivery at scale. With deep expertise in community health programming, institutional capacity building, and digital health innovation, she has led initiatives across African and European contexts, always centering equity, access, and evidence-based practice.

Her current research focuses on developing predictive spatiotemporal frameworks for improving mental health emergency care accessibility, sitting at the intersection of data science, public health, and spatial analysis.

She is also a published author, entrepreneur, and advocate for character education in children, bringing the same rigour and empathy to every domain she enters.
