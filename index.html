About

Where health meets technology

Eloho B. Obukohwo is a multi-disciplinary professional whose work spans global health strategy, pharmacology, health systems strengthening, software development, and education. Based in Sweden, her career bridges the gap between clinical science and the digital tools that can transform healthcare delivery at scale.

With deep expertise in community health programming, institutional capacity building, and digital health innovation, Eloho has led initiatives across African and European contexts — always centering equity, access, and evidence-based practice.

Her current PhD research focuses on developing geospatial-temporal frameworks for improving mental health emergency care accessibility across Swedish regions — work that sits at the intersection of data science, public health, and spatial analysis.

She is also a published author, entrepreneur, and advocate for character education in children, bringing the same rigour and empathy to every domain she enters.
